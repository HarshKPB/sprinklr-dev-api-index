# Business Hours and Holiday Lists
 

In Sprinklr, you can define business hours and holiday lists to specify when your team is available to support customers. This lets you configure official working hours and mark non-working days such as holidays. Once the configuration is created, you can assign it to users directly from the Sprinklr UI.

To access the Business Hours and Holiday Lists configuration page in Sprinklr, navigate to **Launchpad > Platform Modules > Settings > All Settings**, then select **Business Hours**.
