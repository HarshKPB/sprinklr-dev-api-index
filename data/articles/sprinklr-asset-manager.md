# Sprinklr Asset Manager


Sprinklr Asset Manager (SAM) provides an organized and unified repository for you to store, manage, and measure the impact of your content assets. Sprinklr's governance allows you to set content permissions and expiration dates to ensure that the assets you store are consistently used in the right way and that your brand integrity is maintained.

From the Asset Manager, you can create, store, and organize content assets for easy access across the organization.

Using Asset Manger APIs, you can create, fetch, update or even delete an asset.
