# Fetch Ads Objects

The **Fetch Ads Objects** APIs allow you to retrieve details of advertising entities such as ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ad variants, and supporting metadata.
These endpoints help you access both core campaign objects and reference data (audience, placements, catalogs, pixels, and more) required for campaign setup and management.
