# Content Variable APIs

#  Content Variable APIs

The **Content Variable APIs** enable you to create, manage, and maintain reusable placeholders within articles. Content variables are especially useful when specific words or phrases—such as prices, product names, plan descriptions, or region-specific details require frequent updates across multiple articles.

By using content variables, you can define a single placeholder and automatically propagate updates across all associated articles. This ensures consistency, reduces maintenance effort, and improves the accuracy of published content.

## Available Endpoints

- ****[Create Content Variable](https://dev.sprinklr.com/create-content-variable) : Add a new reusable placeholder.

- ****[Fetch Content Variables](https://dev.sprinklr.com/fetch-content-variables): Retrieve a list of all defined variables.

- ****[Fetch Content Variable by ID](https://dev.sprinklr.com/fetch-content-variable-by-id): View the details of a specific variable.

- ****[Delete Content Variable by ID](https://dev.sprinklr.com/delete-content-variable-by-id): Remove a variable when it is no longer needed.

Use these APIs to streamline content updates, enforce consistency, and simplify the management of frequently changing information across your knowledge base.
