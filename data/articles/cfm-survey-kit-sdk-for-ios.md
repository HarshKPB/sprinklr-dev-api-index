# CFM Survey Kit SDK for iOS

# CFM Survey Kit SDK for iOS
 

You can integrate Customer Feedback Management (CFM) in your iOS app to display surveys to users based on certain conditions (intercepts).

**Dev Notes: **To learn more, see [key concepts](https://dev.sprinklr.com/sprinklr-survey-kit-sdk).


## Compatibility Requirements


For iOS integration, ensure your iOS app meets the following minimum requirements:



- **Ruby:** 3.2.1 or later

- **CocoaPods:** 1.14.0 or later

- **iOS:** 15.0 or later


## Integration Steps


The Sprinklr Survey Kit SDK integration involves the following key steps:



- [Install Sprinklr Survey Kit SDK](https://dev.sprinklr.com/install-sprinklr-survey-kit-sdk-for-ios)

- [Initialize Sprinklr Survey Kit SDK](https://dev.sprinklr.com/initialize-sprinklr-survey-kit-sdk-for-ios)

- [Integrate SDK Methods](https://dev.sprinklr.com/sprinklr-survey-kit-sdk-methods-for-ios)


## Advanced Configuration


Once the SDK is integrated, you can enhance the user experience further by enabling advanced configurations:



- [Listen to SDK Events](https://dev.sprinklr.com/survey-events-listener-for-ios): Listen to a comprehensive set of events related to initialization, page visits, button clicks, intercept evaluations, and more, to gain real-time visibility into the lifecycle of intercepts, surveys, and user sessions.

- [Enable Survey Events Logging](https://dev.sprinklr.com/enable-survey-events-logging-for-ios): Enable logging to capture events at various levels based on your requirements. You can log information such as critical errors, warnings, and high-traffic events. This flexible logging system enhances troubleshooting efficiency, helping you monitor app behavior and resolve issues quickly.
