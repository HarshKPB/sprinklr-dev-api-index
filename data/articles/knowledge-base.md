# Knowledge Base

#
  Knowledge Base

	Sprinklr’s unified Knowledge Base helps you deliver a superior self-help experience across the digital channels, thus reducing agent contact volume and empowering customer service teams to leverage proven, consistent information to drive faster resolutions.
You can effortlessly create and manage your knowledge repository using Sprinklr's robust knowledge base builder and empower customers to serve themselves with an SEO-optimized public-facing knowledge base on your website, app, or community. At the same time, agents can reduce resolution time and improve CSAT by using AI-powered Smart Comprehend that suggests the most relevant knowledge base articles directly within an integrated, omnichannel Care Console.

### Use cases of Knowledge Base

- **Brand Care** - Agents can receive recommended articles in Agent Console and Care Console based on the context of customer conversations including the relevant excerpts that they can simply copy and paste as answers.

- **Self Care** - Customers can quickly find answers to most of the queries on a customizable self-service portal with ticketing, live chat, and multilingual support, without the need to contact the brand's support team every time.

- **24x7 Availability** - A knowledge base delivers information around the world 24x7 and ensures accessibility of information doesn't become an obstacle in customers' way.
