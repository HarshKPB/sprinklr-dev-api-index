# Dashboard

#
Dashboard


 Dashboard API allows fetching the list of Sprinklr reporting and listening dashboards and its corresponding metadata. Here's an overview of each of the dashboards:

### Listening Dashboards

Listening Dashboards enable you to analyze topic queries via widgets within Standard and Custom Dashboards, and via a Consumption Dashboard that records the consumption of data across sources and topic queries. You can visualize data in the form of charts and graphs in widgets.

**Related Knowledge Base Article:** **[Listening Dashboards](https://help.sprinklr.com/articles/listening/listening-overview-and-insights-dashboards/6137656052911d3e29ad74c3)**

### Reporting Dashboards

Reporting Insights in Sprinklr centralizes data from all your social channels and accounts. In Reporting Insights, you can customize, expand, and drill into metrics to discover what's working and what’s not, and develop data-based strategies to continuously improve your content performance.

**Related Knowledge Base Article:** **[Reporting Dashboards](https://help.sprinklr.com/articles/reporting/about-the-reporting-dashboards/6137664f52911d3e29ad76a8)**
