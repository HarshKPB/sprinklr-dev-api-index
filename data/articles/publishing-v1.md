# Publishing v1

#
Publishing v1

You can create drafts and publish content across social media and messaging channels using Sprinklr. To ensure that the content reaches your target audience, Sprinklr allows you to:

- Define campaigns under which you want to categorize the posts

- Schedule drafts for publishing at a later date or time

- Customize posts based on channels. For instance, creating carousels or location tagging for Instagram

- Dark post support for creating content for targeting different audiences without publishing the content to the page

- Define different permission or approval checkpoints before publish posts and content
