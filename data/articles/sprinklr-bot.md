# Sprinklr Bot

#
Sprinklr Bot


	Sprinklr’s Conversational AI is an automated AI-powered virtual assistant that can engage with customers. It deciphers the intention of your customer's query, and after identifying the reason they are reaching out, it takes suitable actions as configured by the brand.

Conversational AI also understands the underlying sentiment of your customer and replies appropriately depending on whether it is positive, negative, or neutral. With Trigger Bot Application API, you can trigger Sprinklr’s conversational AI bot and gather all insights into the current flow of conversation. It also helps fetch the current status of the conversation.
