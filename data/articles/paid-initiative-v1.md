# Paid Initiative v1

#
  Paid Initiative

Sprinklr Modern Advertising enables brands to deliver personalized advertising to consumers on modern channels effectively and efficiently with limited risk to their brand using AI, automation and integrations.

It offers a unified platform for teams to plan omnichannel campaigns, execute on campaign strategy, analyze real-time reports, and automatically optimize for improved results.

Paid media initiative is a branched out version of advertising that allows creating paid campaigns for various channels. You can set a budget, objective, and even start and end date for the paid initiative.
