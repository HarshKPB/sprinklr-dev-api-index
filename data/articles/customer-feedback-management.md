# Customer Feedback Management (CFM)

Sprinklr Insights’ Customer Feedback Management (CFM) module is a comprehensive solution that enables organizations to listen, learn, and act on customer feedback at every stage of their customer journey.

With CFM, businesses can capture real-time feedback, streamline workflows, and extract actionable insights to enhance customer satisfaction, drive product development, and improve overall business performance.



The following APIs support automation within the CFM solution.


- **[CFM Workflow API](https://dev.sprinklr.com/cfm-workflow-api)**: Create or update a customer profile using this API and leverage the parameters to trigger personalized survey distributions across Email, SMS or WhatsApp.

- **[CFM Survey Response APIs](https://dev.sprinklr.com/cfm-survey-response-apis)**: Import survey responses from external sources into Sprinklr CFM using API-based integration. Once imported, you can fetch, search, update, and delete responses.

- **[CFM Transaction APIs](https://dev.sprinklr.com/cfm-transaction-apis)**: Transactions allow you to record interaction data for customer events. Create, fetch, or delete transactions

- **[Generate Personalized Survey Link](https://dev.sprinklr.com/generate-personalized-survey-link)**:Generate personalized survey links based on transaction details.

**Dev Note:** CFM must be enabled in your environment before you can utilize these APIs. Please contact your Success Manager for more details.
