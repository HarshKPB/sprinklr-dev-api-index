# CFM Survey Response APIs
 

The Survey Builder module of CFM enables you to create fully customized surveys using a wide range of pre-built templates and a comprehensive template library. You can quickly select templates tailored to your needs or create surveys from scratch with customizable questions, themes, and branding options. The module provides a seamless way to gather feedback, ensuring that surveys align with business requirements and deliver actionable insights in a short timeframe.

Once the survey is created, you can use the Survey Response APIs to create responses for the survey, search for survey responses, fetch specific responses, update them, or delete survey responses.
