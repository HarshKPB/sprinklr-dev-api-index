# Topic Group 2.0

#
  Topic Group



[Topic Groups](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/using-topics/how-to-set-up-and-use-topic-groups/640f109f7517d84a3aaf48f7) allow users a way to organize Topics into specific categories, offering customizable methods of Topic arrangement. In Social Listening, you are able to filter by the Topic Groups you create. You can also enable or disable data fetching for all Topics in a Topic Group.

Creating Topic Groups will help you to organize your social listening and make it more efficient. Using Sprinklr Topic Group APIs you can Create, Read, Update and, Delete topic groups programmatically.
