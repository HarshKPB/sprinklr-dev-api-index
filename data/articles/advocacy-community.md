# Advocacy Community

#
Advocacy Community

 An Advocacy Community is a members-only space for your brand's fans or employee advocates, where they can access exclusive branded content and share it across their social channels. You can manage the advocacy community through the Sprinklr platform.

**Note:** Advertising Marketing should be enabled in your environment before you explore these APIs. Kindly reach out to your success manager for details.

### Advocacy Community Features

Common advocacy community features include:










| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Communities | When you navigate to Advocacy Community, you'll see all of your saved Communities and their information |
| Build | Click on Build to access and edit the build configuration of a Community |
| Settings | Click Settings to access and edit the settings of a Community |
| Options | The Options icon in the lower right corner of a saved Community opens the action menu options where you can Delete an existing Community |
| Add Community | Click Add Community to create a new Community Configuration |
