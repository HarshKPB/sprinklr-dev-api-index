# Disable or Enable Live Chat

Depending on your requirement, you can disable or enable the Live Chat app on your website.

## Disable Live Chat

This method disables the Live Chat app completely on the website. The user will not see the Live Chat app on the website and no SDK commands will be executed.

**Dev Notes:** This method does not change session/user, and everything is preserved.

### Method

`sprChat(‘disable’);`

## Enable Live Chat

This method enables the Live Chat app. After the Live Chat app is enabled, customers can see the Live Chat widget on their screen, provided all the other visibility conditions configured in the Live Chat builder are met.

### Method

`sprChat(‘enable’);`
