# Live Chat SDKs

# Live Chat SDKs

Live Chat is a Sprinklr Service feature that enables chat support on your websites or mobile applications. You can fully customize the Live Chat support according to your brand requirements.

With Live Chat, your customers can communicate with you and get their questions answered quickly. Your customers can also have video and audio calls with the support agents and attach documents with their queries, ensuring faster resolution. To know more, see [Live Chat](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/setup-care-operations/live-chat/63a3221a70271619c87d9da2).

## Live Chat SDK

Live Chat SDK is a collection of methods for Live Chat extensibility. SDK methods give you complete control over how the Live Chat support works on your website or mobile app. You can define how customers can open and close the chat window, what information must be shown when the chat window opens, and more.

## Benefits of using Live Chat SDK

With Live Chat SDK, you can do the following:

- **Meet your brand requirements**: You can define how your Live Chat support looks, behaves, and more.

- **Enhance customer experience**: You can leverage SDK methods to enhance the user experience. For example, you can show the Knowledge Base list when the chat window opens. This self-serve option will help the customer get answers to their queries quickly.

- **Leverage advance capabilities**: You can leverage capabilities such as user authentication​, external events,​ dark/light mode, update locale, and more.

## Supported platforms and frameworks

The Live Chat SDK methods are supported on the following platforms and frameworks:

**Website**

HTML, JavaScript, TypeScript

**Mobile**

- Native applications

- React Native

- Flutter

- WebView
