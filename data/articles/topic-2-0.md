# Topic 2.0

#
  Topic



Sprinklr Topic APIs empowers you to programmatically Create, Read, Update and, Delete topics using the APIs. Topics are the fundamental units to listen within Sprinklr. They consist of queries, constructed with keywords or [Keyword Lists](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/entities/introduction-to-keyword-lists/64634db78ea3c9635cf3673d), boolean operators, filters and sources. 

You can add the created topics to a [Listening Dashboard](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/listening-dashboards/create-a-listening-dashboard-to-listen-engage-and-respond-to-conversations/6808fcadcbfca249dfef594d),  [Listening Widgets](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/listening-dashboards/create-listening-dashboard-widgets-to-visualize-data-in-realtime/6808fd11cffeb5201c6cffbf)or [a Listening Column in the Engagement Dashboard](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/threads/create-a-threads-column-in-the-engagement-dashboard/65a63e3bd39ae90f841f971c) to cater to your listening requirements. For optimum use, first structure and plan your listening requirement, and then with the help of [Topics](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/topics/introduction-to-listening-topics/6405ee7b7a695d65a1606b79) configure the dashboards to get rich insights.
