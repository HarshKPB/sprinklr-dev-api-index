# v1 Case

#
v1 Case


Sprinklr's Case Management helps your teams keep up with the volume of daily messages, meet SLA requirements, and resolve issues for your customers. By bundling related inbound and outbound messages into Cases, your team members have all the information they need at their fingertips. Equipped with background information on your customers' issues and questions, your care agents can provide the best possible service, addressing questions and issues comprehensively and efficiently and providing real solutions.

Case Management keeps related messages bundled together so that agents can see all messages that relate to a Case and all of the steps that have been taken to resolve it. This makes it much easier for various agents to collaborate on one Case, with all agents having a comprehensive view of the entire conversation workflow.

Instead of tagging each message, agents only need to label the Case once, simplifying the workflow for Cases that may include many messages. Furthermore, bundling messages into cases provides more accurate reporting about the number of issues the customer service team has dealt with rather than the number of messages they have received and sent.

## The Value of Case Management

Many customer service teams receive an extremely high volume of inbound messages, many of which are multiple messages relating to the same issue. A customer, for example, may send out multiple Tweets and Facebook posts expressing their frustration. Sprinklr's Case Management APIs allow service teams to fetch, update, and delete cases.
