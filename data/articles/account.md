# Account

#
Account

An account refers to the brand’s social media account that has been successfully added to the Sprinklr. Once the account has been added to the Sprinklr platform, you can view, edit, update, delete, or deactivate the account.

**Related Knowledge Base Article:** **[Accounts and Account Groups](https://help.sprinklr.com/articles/account/accounts-and-account-groups/613766475f2e7c0c9ed36d11)**

To access accounts on Sprinklr’s platform —> open launchpad from the top-left corner → click on All Settings → Click on the Manage Workspace option on the left → Click on the Accounts icon
