# Content Template

Content Template Builder powers cross-channel content management which lets users easily consolidate their desired images, videos, and content across non-social channels like Websites, Blogs, Emails, etc.

With content template APIs, you can easily fetch details for user-created as well as standard content templates. Moreover, you can also fetch content template details based on applied filters and channel type.
