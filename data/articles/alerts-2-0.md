# Alerts 2.0

# Listening Alerts

Listening Alerts are notifications designed to inform users about unusual or important activity across your brand’s digital channels. There are two types of Listening Alerts:


- **Smart Alerts**: Powered by Sprinklr Intuition, Smart Alerts analyze conversations around your brand and alert your team when anomalies are detected on metrics of your choice. Based on historical data, the algorithm recognizes trends in selected metrics like mention volume, engagement, sentiment, and more.

- **Volumetric (Static) Alerts**:Volumetric Alerts provide a configurable, rule-based alerting mechanism that notifies you when predefined thresholds are breached. These alerts are useful when you want to monitor absolute values or rate changes in key listening metrics.


Using Listening Alerts APIs, you can programmatically create, update, retrieve, and delete Smart or Volumetric alerts.
