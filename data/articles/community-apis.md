# Community APIs

#
  Community APIs

Communities is a support tool that helps brands communicate more effectively and efficiently. It helps in building a connection between individuals by bringing one or more attributes together. You can use communities for customer feedback, to improve brand recognition, to test the viability of ideas and new products, or to mobilize customer loyalty.

**Related Knowledge Base Article: ** **[Sprinklr Communities](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/categories/community/63a2a83a354db705e07a18be)**

**Dev Notes: **The Community APIs are designed to support limited and specific use cases only. They are not intended for building or replicating a full-scale community platform. For more advanced or large-scale community features, please contact your Sprinklr representative to explore supported solutions.

## Use Cases of Sprinklr Communities

### 1. Peer Care

- Customers being able to self-serve by asking and answering questions and sharing ideas with each other

- A group of trusted peers giving each other advice and suggestions

### 2. Valuable Insights

- Seamless exchange of knowledge, ideas, and clarifications (questions/answers)

- Can be used as a source of new ideas to make better services/products for your users

### 3. Increased Customer Loyalty

- Helps foster a positive relationship between your brand and customers as customers feel heard and important

- Being a source of feedback, you can understand the strengths and weaknesses of your products

### 4. Improved Support Process

- Acts as an additional support channel

- Customers answering each other questions helps in the reduced number of support tickets

**Dev Note:**Please note that Community APIs have a different authorization process, which requires access to projectId and preSharedKey for generating unauthenticated and authenticated token. Please reach out to your success manager for getting started with Community APIs.
