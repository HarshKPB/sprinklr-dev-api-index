# Product APIs v1

#
  Product APIs

Sprinklr offers a variety of robust, convenient, and simple RESTful Product APIs that allow users to create, update, and delete products. You can create products with product-specific sources and accounts using the Create Product API. Once created, product insights provide valuable data and analysis of customer experiences based on industry-specific customer reviews.

Product APIs allow you to create a product and track performance against leaders in the industry to see how your brand measures up, providing valuable data that can help make decisions and develop a strategy based on the full picture.
