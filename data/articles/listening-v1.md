 Listening


	Sprinklr's **Listening** allows you to instantly understand the trends, topics, and influencers driving the market and helps you understand how your brand can stand out.
**Listening** empowers you to filter through the noise for more breadth, depth, and actionability than ever. Even better, you're doing it with fewer restrictions so you can explore your data and your audiences more freely and completely.
